# CityReport.io Finance Package

Prepared: July 20, 2026  
Status: Internal working baseline; leadership, legal, tax, and MOU review required before customer use

## Purpose

This directory is the canonical working package for CityReport.io pricing, cost control, billing operations, and finance ownership.

## Documents

- `FINANCE_TEAM_CHARTER.md` — fractional finance team, authority, cadence, controls, and immediate work plan.
- `PRICING_AND_COST_MODEL.md` — proposed municipal pricing, Ashtabula commercial position, verified vendor pricing, cost scenarios, gross-margin rules, and invoice-intake checklist.

## Current executive recommendation

1. Do not build a full-time finance department yet. Use a founder-led fractional team with bookkeeping, CPA, municipal contracts counsel, Sales/Revenue Operations, and Engineering/FinOps support.
2. Sell annual municipal contracts, billed in advance through approved invoice/PO/ACH or check workflows. Do not make self-service card billing the default.
3. Use population, active production domains, named staff seats, and managed assets as scope guardrails. Never charge residents per account or per report.
4. Use a `$4,500` 60–90 day Validation Pilot and a `$12,000` annual Launch package as the starting commercial position for a city the size of Ashtabula, subject to the signed MOU.
5. Protect at least `80%` subscription gross margin and require written finance approval below `70%`.
6. Collect actual invoices and trailing usage before treating any vendor estimate as an actual current expense.

## Important limitation

No MOU document or vendor invoices were found in the repository. The pricing model is a decision-ready recommendation, not confirmation of what the MOU permits or what vendor accounts are currently charging.
