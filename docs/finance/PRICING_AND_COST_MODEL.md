# CityReport.io Pricing and Cost Model

Prepared: July 20, 2026  
Currency: USD, before tax  
Status: Internal recommendation; not an approved quote or legal/tax advice

## 1. Executive decision

Use an annual municipal SaaS contract with a one-time implementation fee. The controlling package is the highest scope dimension reached across:

- Population or service-area band
- Active production domains/workflows
- Named internal staff accounts
- Managed GIS assets

Resident accounts and civic issue submissions are unmetered. Capacity limits are commercial guardrails, not surprise overages. Any expansion is quoted, approved in writing, prorated, and co-termed.

This preserves the repository's earlier direction—annual base fee by active domain plus named staff seats—while adding population and managed assets so one package cannot absorb a much larger operational footprint without repricing.

## 2. Definitions

### Active production domain

A municipal workflow enabled for real production use by the contracted tenant. A disabled domain retained only for historical records does not count.

### Named staff account

A person with active access to a tenant staff workspace. Multiple roles within one tenant count once. Access to separately contracted municipalities counts once per municipality. Resident/public accounts are never billable staff seats.

### Managed asset

A city or utility asset record imported, hosted, displayed, or maintained by CityReport.io. Report-only workflows without a maintained asset inventory do not consume an asset allowance.

### Tenant COGS

Tenant-variable infrastructure, maps, storage, email/SMS, payment fees, monitoring, recurring integration maintenance, and support/customer-success labor. Codex, general product development, broad marketing, accounting, and general legal work are operating expenses unless directly attributable to a customer commitment.

## 5. Ashtabula founding-city position

The correct city spelling is **Ashtabula**. The U.S. Census Bureau's 2025 estimate is `17,474`, placing the city in the Launch population band. Source: [U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/ashtabulacityohio/PST045225).

### List economics

- Validation Pilot: `$4,500` for up to 90 days
- Steady-state Launch subscription: `$12,000/year`
- Standard implementation: `$4,000`

### Recommended conversion option

- Credit `50%` of a paid pilot fee toward the first annual subscription if the city signs within 30 days of pilot closeout.
- A documented Founding City discount may reduce the subscription by up to `20%` for the first two years only, but it requires Founder + Finance approval because it exceeds the normal discount guardrail.
- Waive implementation only if the configuration remains standard and the value of the waiver is shown on the order form.

A 20% subscription discount reduces Launch ARR to `$9,600`. At the full `$2,400` Launch COGS ceiling, gross margin would be `75%`, below the `80%` target. Preserve 80% margin only if annual tenant COGS is at or below `$1,920`; otherwise record a formal margin exception or reduce the discount.

### If the signed MOU requires a free pilot

- Record `$4,500` as the in-kind pilot value.
- Preserve the 90-day, two-domain, eight-staff, 5,000-asset, standard-support, and no-custom-development limits.
- Do not imply that free pilot access establishes a perpetual free price.
- Put conversion pricing and the decision date in a separate approved document if the MOU allows it.

### MOU review gate

No MOU was found in the repository. Counsel and Finance must review the signed document for:

- Pilot term, scope, extensions, and termination
- Whether fees or later conversion discussions are permitted
- Procurement and purchase-order requirements
- Data ownership, retention, export, and deletion
- Security, insurance, indemnity, and service commitments
- Publicity, city logo, case study, testimonial, and reference rights
- Non-appropriation and budget-cycle language

Do not assume the MOU grants permission to use the city name or logo in marketing.

## 6. Contract and billing rules

- Annual subscription billed in advance, normally Net 30.
- Implementation invoiced separately at signing or against defined milestones.
- One-year default term; optional three-year agreement with annual increases capped at 3%.
- One-year renewal increases capped at 5% unless scope changes.
- Expansion is written, approved, prorated, and co-termed.
- No automatic usage overage invoices.
- Scope review begins 60–90 days before renewal.
- Customer data export is available during the term and for a contract-defined transition period after expiration.
- Use municipal invoice, PO, ACH, or check processes first; Stripe is optional, not required.
- Payment, tax exemption, non-appropriation, records retention, security, termination, and governing law require counsel/CPA review.
- The platform does not guarantee municipal response, repair, report accuracy, or emergency handling.
- No SLA is promised without Engineering and Legal approval.

## 7. Gross-margin model

Target subscription gross margin: `80%`.

Hard review threshold: `70%`.

Formula:

```text
minimum ARR = projected annual tenant COGS / (1 - target gross margin)
```

Example:

```text
$2,400 annual tenant COGS / (1 - 80%) = $12,000 minimum ARR
```

### Annual tenant COGS ceilings

| Package | COGS ceiling at 80% target | Monthly equivalent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Launch | `$2,400` | `$200` |
| Operations | `$4,800` | `$400` |
| Regional | `$8,400` | `$700` |

Additional controls:

- Standard implementation services target at least `40%` gross margin.
- Custom integrations target at least `50%` gross margin.
- Reserve `3% of ARR` for vendor spikes and incident response.
- Alert Finance when tenant COGS reaches `15% of ARR`.
- Require corrective pricing or scope action when tenant COGS reaches `20% of ARR`.
- Standard support labor guardrails are about 12, 30, and 72 hours annually for Launch, Operations, and Regional.

These are management assumptions for budgeting, not researched vendor quotes. Obtain written scopes before engagement.

## 12. Cost formulas for the first 100,000 monthly events

Use these only within the stated pricing bands:

```text
Dynamic Maps cost = MAX(0, monthly map loads - 10,000) / 1,000 * $7
Geocoding cost = MAX(0, monthly geocoding events - 10,000) / 1,000 * $5
Nearest Roads cost = MAX(0, monthly road events - 5,000) / 1,000 * $10
Resend Pro overage = MAX(0, monthly emails - 50,000) / 1,000 * $0.90
Minimum ARR at 80% GM = annual tenant COGS / 20%
```

Google applies tiered/volume pricing, so replace these simple formulas above 100,000 events with the current official tier calculation.
